# Kitabi UI/UX review — 28 Jul 2026

A senior-designer pass over every screen in `app/lib/features/`, reviewed against
[screen-design.md](screen-design.md) (the design constitution) and the
`kitabi_screens.html` mockups. Findings are code-verified with `file:line` cites.
Severity: **HIGH** = actively confuses or breaks the experience · **MED** =
inconsistency or friction · **LOW** = polish.

The app's voice is strong and mostly consistent — the bookplate profile, the
`BorrowerField` no-dead-end flow, the recs "thinking" loader, and the stop-sheet
copy are reference-quality. What follows is where the rest should catch up.

---

## Part 1 — The ten highest-leverage fixes

1. **Dark mode is broken wherever a brightness-aware token meets a constant-dark
   (or constant-light) surface.** Four real breakages found:
   - Apple sign-in button: `backgroundColor: AppColors.ink` + theme foreground
     `dark ? ink : paper` → cream-on-cream at night (`sign_in_screen.dart:93`,
     `app_theme.dart:85`).
   - QuoteCard: quote text `AppColors.goldSoft` on the always-dark panel → dark
     brown on dark brown (`core/widgets/quote_card.dart:57`).
   - Notes "slip paper": hardcoded light hexes (`0xFFF6EEDC`/`0xFFE8DCC0`) with
     `AppColors.ink` text → cream-on-cream at night (`book_detail_screen.dart:3578`,
     `notes_journal_screen.dart:118`, `note_page.dart:220`).
   - Share cards rasterise in the *current* theme, so the outward-facing gold-framed
     "aged paper" brand card ships as a near-black tile from dark mode
     (`share_capture.dart` + all three cards).
   **Rule to adopt:** anything on a constant surface uses constant colors. Fix the
   four, then audit every `AppColors.night`/hardcoded-hex pairing.

2. **`fontFamily: 'Fraunces'` never renders Fraunces — 8 call sites.** No font is
   bundled in `pubspec.yaml` and google_fonts registers its own family names, so the
   serif numerals on the note page's live clock, the session page-entry numeral, the
   stop sheet, and two picker sheets silently fall back to the default sans
   (`session_page_entry.dart:210,221,396`, `note_page.dart:404,567`,
   `stop_session_sheet.dart:433`, `work_picker_screen.dart:438`,
   `chip_picker_sheet.dart:136`). Replace with `GoogleFonts.fraunces(...)` or theme
   text styles.

3. **The username field fights its own validation.** The keyboard capitalizes every
   first letter (`TextCapitalization.words`, `profile_screen.dart:733`) while the
   regex demands lowercase-first (`^[a-z]…`, :652) — every attempt starts invalid and
   the visible text never matches what's validated. Use `.none` + a lowercase
   formatter.

4. **Network failure impersonates the empty state across the catalog cluster —
   and the empty copy invites duplicates.** Work/author/publisher pickers and the
   ISBN scanner all catch-and-blank: offline, the reader sees "No matches — add a
   new one" (`work_picker_screen.dart:79`, `author_picker_screen.dart:94`,
   `publisher_picker_screen.dart:74`) or "No book found for that ISBN"
   (`isbn_scan_screen.dart:62`). This defeats duplicate pressure — the cluster's
   central design idea. Distinguish error from empty everywhere, with retry.

5. **The four progress surfaces have drifted again — plus a fifth.**
   `SessionPageEntry` unified the timer stop face and quick-stop sheet, but:
   - `_ManualLogSheet` never adopted it — plain TextFields, total crammed beside
     page, no −/+, no validation, and it pre-fills the end page with the current
     page so an untouched save logs a zero-page sitting
     (`book_detail_screen.dart:2705–2822`).
   - Home's "+" progress dialog is a bare AlertDialog with no validation, no total
     capture, no select-all (`home_screen.dart:588–617`).
   - The timer's own stop face lags the quick-stop sheet: no "last time" anchor
     line, no link into the reading log, and **no notes section at all** — the most
     deliberate stop path loses the closing-thought moment and the "your notes are
     already saved" reassurance (`reading_timer_screen.dart:898–917` vs
     `stop_session_sheet.dart:244–317`).
   Route all of them through `SessionPageEntry` + the shared notes block, and add a
   call-site checklist comment on the widget.

6. **Recommendations opt-in retry is wired to the wrong provider.** The error
   branch watches `recsOptInProvider` but retry invalidates
   `recommendationsProvider` (`recommendations_screen.dart:37`) — tapping Retry
   does nothing. One-line fix; the whole feature is unreachable behind a failed
   opt-in fetch until then.

7. **The add form can lose everything to one swipe, and warns about edits too
   late.** No unsaved-changes guard on back/edge-swipe (`add_edit_book_screen.dart:1298`
   — add a `PopScope` "Discard this book?"), and editing someone else's Work shows
   no gold banner before typing (M5 spec) while the sticky bar still claims "Saved
   books join the shared catalog" on the revision path (`:1798`).

8. **The review editor can't delete, and lies about saving.** Clearing the text or
   the stars and tapping Save silently keeps the old review/rating, then toasts
   "Review saved" and pops (`review_editor_screen.dart:77–99`). Treat emptied body
   as delete (or add an explicit delete), let a second tap clear a star, toast what
   actually happened — and pop after the *local* write instead of awaiting a full
   sync round-trip (offline-first).

9. **Lending's ledger misstates linked borrows and grew a third tab.** Every
   borrowed row says "Self-logged — just for your own tracking" unconditionally
   (`lending_ledger_screen.dart:739`) — inverted for linked lenders; gate on
   `borrowerUserId == null` and show the gold 🔗 badge otherwise. And the
   always-visible "Rejected · 0" tab breaks "two tabs, one ledger" (`:134`) — show
   it only when non-empty, or fold rejected into Lent-out as a stamped section.

10. **The activity feed never names the book.** Every row is a generic verb +
    relative date (`activity_screen.dart:137–143`); the route resolver already
    fetches the entity on tap — resolve it in the row and lead with a small
    `TypesetCover` + title ("Finished *Aadujeevitham*"). This screen is the seed of
    the future community feed; a feed with no subjects won't grow into one.

## Part 2 — Systemic patterns (fix once, apply everywhere)

- **Two missing tokens cause most color drift.** Add to `AppColors`:
  - `darkPanel = Color(0xFF3A2C1E)` — the spec's "single dark accent card". Screens
    currently reach for `AppColors.night` (the scanner color) instead
    (`insights_screen.dart:493`, `period_summary_card.dart:284`, Home's three dark
    slabs). Reserve `night` for the scanner.
  - `goldInk = Color(0xFF8F681E)` — the darker gold used for text on `goldSoft`;
    currently pasted in ≥5 files, and `AppColors.gold` on `goldSoft` fails contrast
    (~2.4:1) in `time_to_finish.dart:343,488,569`. Also `slip`/`slipLine` tokens for
    the notes paper, and a `nightGold` family for the timer's ~12 hardcoded
    `0xFFE3B14C`s.
- **Section labels are re-implemented ~10 times** at 7.5–11px with drifting
  spacing/weight, casing sometimes in code and sometimes baked into arb strings
  (bad for Malayalam). Promote one `SectionLabel` widget (9px-equiv, uppercase in
  code, letter-spaced, `inkSoft`, w700) to `core/widgets/` and sweep.
- **Raw `$err` reaches readers in ~10 places** (add/edit form ×4, add-edition,
  both pickers, import, log-borrowed sheet, book page Yours tab). One shared quiet
  error helper — plain sentence + brief detail — plus `ErrorRetry` for full-screen
  cases. Also: several `catch (_) {}` swallow failures entirely (language picker
  save, revision withdraw, profile sheets) — standardize on the sign-in SnackBar
  pattern.
- **Busy flags without try/finally** brick primary buttons on a thrown await
  (`lend_sheet._save`, `import._import`). The share sheets do it right; copy that
  shape.
- **Tap targets routinely fall below 44px**: 16px rating stars
  (`book_detail_screen.dart:1228`), the ~24px stop-sheet Skip, note-page header
  close/save, search "Clear", browse quick-add badge (mis-taps open the book),
  oxblood name-links. A single hit-area pass materially improves the quiet UI
  without changing its look.
- **Touch feedback is split ~50/50** between `InkWell` (ripple) and bare
  `GestureDetector` (nothing). Pick one policy — a subtle highlight suits paper
  better than Material ripple — via one shared wrapper.
- **Instant irreversible taps with no undo**: mark-returned, recs dismiss/wishlist,
  disconnect, shelf remove, sign out. House standard: SnackBar-with-Undo (quiet,
  no dialogs) except sign-out and disconnect, which deserve a confirm.
- **Plurals**: "your 1 notes are already saved" (`app_localizations_en.dart:2254`),
  "1 notes across 1 sittings" (journal), "Due in 0d" (should read "Due today").
  Convert to ICU plurals.
- **"catalog" vs "catalogue"** split brain across `app_en.arb` — pick one and sweep.
- **Duplicated private forks that will drift** (the CLAUDE.md lesson, live again):
  `add_edition_screen`'s wholesale copy of the form's field widgets (already
  drifted); two "new shelf" dialogs with different capitalization; two segmented-
  control implementations (underline TabBar vs pill); four hand-rolled sheet
  grabbers next to the shared `SheetGrabber`; two share-glyph copies; three private
  English date formatters. Extract shared widgets.

## Part 3 — Per-screen findings

### Splash, onboarding, sign-in, update gate
- [MED] Welcome titles are oxblood — the action color on non-tappable headings, on
  the very first screens; use `ink` (`welcome_screen.dart:82`).
- [MED] `signInQuote3` is untranslated German Kafka with no attribution
  (`app_en.arb:33`); use the English rendering QuoteCard already has.
- [MED] Sign-in quote renders in Inter italic; spec says quotes are Fraunces italic
  (`sign_in_screen.dart:75`). Add the mockup's gold fleuron `❦` between tagline and
  quote while there.
- [MED] Language picker: failed save is silent (`catch (_)`,
  `language_picker_screen.dart:39`) on a screen the user cannot leave — SnackBar it;
  and hint why Continue is disabled ("Pick at least one").
- [MED] Update gate says "App Store" to Android users (`app_en.arb:1685`); make it
  platform-aware, lead with `BrandMark` not `Icons.system_update`, and add a
  "Try again" re-check.
- [LOW] Splash hardcodes 'Kitabi' (skip l10n); reduced-motion freezes the loader
  dots to near-invisible `line` grey; brand line appears as both "Beyond the
  bookshelf" and "…the Bookshelf" — one casing.

### Home
- [MED] Currently-reading is a dark slab (mockup draws it light with an oxblood
  bar), so Home shows 3+ dark panels where the constitution allows one
  (`home_screen.dart:443`). Restyle to `card`, or at least use the new `darkPanel`
  token for all.
- [MED] QuoteCard duplicates the three arb quotes as hardcoded literals and sets
  them in Inter (`quote_card.dart:25–29,55`); point at l10n, use Fraunces italic.
- [MED] The progress dialog needs "of N pages" context + range validation (see
  Part 1 #5).
- [LOW] Two nearby "+" glyphs mean update-progress and add-a-book — use the pencil
  for progress; lending nudge has a chevron but no labelled action; pull-to-refresh
  completes instantly (`ref.invalidate` not awaited — use `ref.refresh(...).future`);
  the static gold "live" dot should reuse `PulsingDot`.

### Profile & settings
- [MED] Your avatar is missing the gold ring + Fraunces initial that the linked-
  reader language derives from (`profile_screen.dart:149`; mockup 12).
- [MED] Five stacked outlined buttons → one card of chevron rows (matches the
  Languages card); sign out needs a confirm.
- [LOW] "Reading since 2026" drops the mockup's "· 128 books"; six grey zero-pills
  greet a new reader in Reputation — hide zeros; `_vis` seeded once and never
  re-seeded on profile refresh; spinner color `AppColors.paper` inverts in dark
  mode (3 sites).

### Library grid & filter sheet
- [MED] `ShelfCover` overlays: hardcoded English ('RETURNED', 'WITH …') and 5.5–7px
  type — unreadable decoration; localize and raise to ≥8.5px
  (`core/widgets/shelf_cover.dart:103–152`).
- [MED] Filter sheet status chips drop the per-status ink vocabulary — tint
  selected chips with `readingStatusInk` (`library_filter_sheet.dart:341`).
- [LOW] Sort-sheet title is 13px Inter while sibling sheets use Fraunces
  `titleLarge`; "Favourites only" is the lone Switch in an all-chip sheet; pin the
  "Show N books" button outside the scroll; `_NewShelfTile` mixes gold border with
  oxblood content — pick one accent.

### Book page
- [MED] `_Stars` rounds 3.5 → 4 stars everywhere the community average appears —
  render half-stars (`book_detail_screen.dart:580`).
- [MED] Rating chevron renders even when not tappable (unowned books, `:543`);
  16px your-rating stars need real hit areas (`:1228`); the permanent trash can
  sits beside share/favourite — move into the overflow menu (`:1434`).
- [MED] Review body is Inter italic while a lending note below gets Fraunces —
  reviews are *the* specified home of the literary voice (`:3178` vs `:4210`).
- [MED] Yours-tab error prints the raw exception (`:191`) — use `ErrorRetry`.
- [LOW] "Four states, one tap each." narrates the widget — delete; About-tab genre
  chips are outlined against the "tints, not outlines" rule; Yours/About underline
  tabs vs the app's pill segments; shelf "Remove" is the only silent shelf
  mutation — toast it.

- [LOW] Unselected radio drawn in `line` (~1.4:1) — use `inkSoft`; Done button
  re-declares the theme style inline.

## Part 4 — Bright spots (protect these)

- `BorrowerField`'s "keep as private contact" no-match row — the "never a dead
  end" ideal, executed.
- The `_RecsThinkingLoader` (breathing gold rings, reduced-motion handling,
  fleuron) — the reference AI-wait state.
- The public-profile bookplate (Ex Libris eyebrow, gold hairline inset, rotated
  corner stamp) — the strongest single expression of the Reading Room voice.
- The stop sheet's consequence-naming copy and `SessionPageEntry`'s −/+ anchor
  design — right idea; the remaining work is adoption (Part 1 #5).

## Suggested sequencing

1. **Correctness-adjacent UX** (a day or two): #1 dark-mode four, #3 username,
   #6 recs retry, #9 self-logged label, review-editor delete, error-vs-empty in
   pickers/scanner.
2. **Token + shared-widget pass**: `darkPanel`/`goldInk`/`slip`/`nightGold`,
   `SectionLabel`, quiet-error helper, SnackBar-with-Undo, hit-area sweep.
3. **Flow work**: progress-surface unification (#5), add-form guard + M5 banner
   (#7), lending tab/FAB rework, activity rows naming books.
4. **Polish**: Fraunces/fleuron/copy sweeps, chart labels, share-card logo +
   capture resolution.
