The reading check-in, on the reader's clock

The "still reading?" nudge fires a fixed 60 minutes into every sitting — too eager for a long Sunday read, and nobody's to change. The default moves to 2 hours and the interval becomes a per-reader setting on Profile, where Dark mode and Promotions already live. The 30-minute answer window after the nudge stays fixed: it's the safety net's spring, not a preference.

The setting

One card on Profile, one sheet

Same grammar as every Profile setting: a card row stating the current value, opening a bottom sheet of radio options — the single-select list the shelf picker and status sheet already taught. Five intervals, no "Off": the check-in exists so a timer forgotten under a falling-asleep reader doesn't log a nine-hour sitting, and a safety net you can switch off isn't one. The longest option (4 hours) is generous enough that nobody who means to be reading ever meets the nudge. Stored device-local in key_values like the reading goal and theme — the timer is device-local by design, so its policy is too.
